Plumbing work we do a lot of in Cwmbran

Cwmbran was built in the late 1940s as one of Wales' first new towns — and a big chunk of the housing stock has been ticking over on the same heating systems ever since. We work across Llantarnam, Thornhill, Oakfield, Greenmeadow and pretty much everywhere in between. A few things come up again and again:

Boiler replacements and combi upgrades

A lot of properties still have older back boilers or system boilers with separate cylinders and tanks in the loft. Swapping over to a modern combi is one of the best upgrades you can make — smaller, more efficient, and you lose all that pipework in the airing cupboard. We'll quote the whole job before starting and handle the Gas Safe sign-off and benchmark certificate.

Radiator problems

Cold spots at the top of a radiator usually means air in the system — an easy bleed. Cold at the bottom usually means sludge, which needs a power flush or at minimum a chemical clean. We see a lot of systems in Cwmbran where nobody's touched the inhibitor in years. Worth sorting before another winter.

Leaks and dripping taps

They're rarely as minor as they look. We'll trace it properly and fix it once, not just tighten something and hope for the best.
