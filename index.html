What we do

Plumbing & heating, done properly.

Domestic plumbing across Newport, Cwmbran, Caerphilly and the wider South Wales area. If it carries water or gas, we'll fix it, fit it or upgrade it.

Combi boiler installations

New combi installs, swaps and upgrades from trusted brands — Worcester, Vaillant, Ideal. Gas Safe certified with full benchmark sign-off.

Radiators & central heating

New radiator installs, replacements, balancing, power flushes and full central heating system upgrades. Warmer rooms, lower bills.

Leak detection & repair

From a slow drip to a burst pipe. We trace the source, fix it cleanly and make good — no guesswork, no ripping up half the house.

Outside taps

Garden and outside tap installations, fully insulated and isolated, fitted in a couple of hours. Ideal for hosepipes, cars and pressure washers.

Water purification

Whole-house filters and softeners installed and serviced. Better-tasting water, less limescale, longer life on every appliance you own.

24-hour emergency callouts

Out-of-hours emergencies across Newport and South Wales. Burst pipes, no heating, no hot water — call and we'll be on the way.

Common questions

The bits people usually ask.

Do you charge a callout fee?
No. We never charge a callout fee anywhere in Newport or South Wales. You only pay for the work carried out.
Are you Gas Safe registered?
Yes. Ollie is Gas Safe registered and holds NVQ Level 3 in Plumbing and Heating. All gas work is fully certified, benchmarked and insured.
Do you cover emergencies out of hours?
Yes — 24-hour emergency callouts across Newport, Cwmbran, Caerphilly, Cardiff and the wider South Wales area for leaks, burst pipes and heating breakdowns.
What areas do you cover?
Newport, Cwmbran, Caerphilly, Cardiff, Pontypool, Chepstow, Risca, Blackwood, Magor, Caldicot, Abergavenny and the surrounding South Wales postcodes. If you're nearby, ask — we'll let you know.
How quickly can you get to me?
For emergencies in Newport we usually attend within the hour. For booked work we give you a clear time slot, not a vague "between 8 and 5" window.
Do you give written quotes?
Yes. For anything beyond a quick fix you'll get a clear written quote before any work starts. No surprises on the invoice.
